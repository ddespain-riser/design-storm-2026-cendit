## Measurements

All files cover roughly 2022-04-01 to 2026-08-19, daily.

| File | Columns | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `data/FoothillsInfluent.csv` | `DATE, TOC_mg_L, Alk_mg_L` | The prediction target: what arrives at the Foothills plant. ~1115 rows, sparser than the inputs. |
| `data/HoosierPass.csv` | `DATE, SWE` | The snow input the models use. ISO dates, unlike the others. |
| `data/MichiganCreek.csv` | `DATE, SWE` | Superseded. Carries the SWE 9.0 May 12–15 2026 artifact. Do not build on it. |
| `data/SouthPlatteFlow.csv` | `measDate, Flow_CFS` | River flow, DWR gage. |
| `data/SouthPlatteTelemetry.csv` | `Date, Flow_CFS, GageHeight_ft, Precip` | Flow plus stage and precipitation. |
| `data/USC00058022.csv` | `STATION, DATE, PRCP, SNOW, TMAX, TMIN` | GHCN weather station, daily. |
| `data/USGS_South_Platte.csv` | `Date, site_no,` then max/mean/min or max/median/min of `Dissolved_Oxygen`, `Specific_Cond`, `Temp_C`, `Turbidity`, `pH` | USGS gage 06707525, above Strontia. ~1027 rows. The water-quality proxy upstream of the reservoir. |

Dates are `M/D/YYYY` everywhere except `HoosierPass.csv`. Rows are not aligned
across files; join on date and expect holes.

## The system as modelled

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `strontia-brief/places.json` | Named geography, as objects keyed by slug (`turbidity`, `foothills_plant`, …) under `gages`, `reservoirs`, `places`, `conveyance`, `facilities`, `event`, plus `_sources`. Start here for "where is X". |
| `strontia-brief/basins/*.json` | Watershed polygons and river flowlines, named by gage number. |

| `water-system-3d/snotel-history.json`, `storage-history.json` | Full daily SWE and reservoir storage histories, folded per water year with a median. The wet-vs-dry comparison already exists here. |
