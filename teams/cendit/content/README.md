# Content

Material team cendit captured rather than authored: what an SME said, what a
session covered, what a source outside this repo holds.

Denver Water's own originals are not here. They stay exactly where they were sent,
in `data/`, `scripts/`, `figures/`, and `reference/`, and `AGENTS.md` keeps them
read-only. This directory is for the material we gathered ourselves.

## What belongs here

Transcripts, session notes, answers to questions we asked, excerpts we leaned on.
Anything whose value is that it records what someone actually said or what a
source actually contains.

Not our conclusions about any of it. Those go in [`../thinking/`](../thinking/).

## Ground rules

- **Edit for readability, never for meaning.** A cleaned transcript says so at the
  top, names what was changed, and sits beside the raw one.
- **Keep the raw version.** Cleaning is lossy and we do not always know yet which
  detail mattered.
- **Say what you could not hear.** Auto-generated transcripts mangle names and
  numbers. Mark the uncertain spot rather than guessing a plausible word — a
  guessed station name or threshold is exactly the kind of invented number
  `AGENTS.md` forbids.
- **Date it, and say where it came from.** Who was in the room and when.
