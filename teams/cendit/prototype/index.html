Events

Our rule, not Denver Water's (register Q7): a day is a trigger if turbidity daily max is above the chosen percentile of the record, or the 3-day flow rise is. Triggers within the gap merge into one event.

    Stratification (register Q18)

    Read this first

    • Strontia has 4 gates at different heights (Jake). The defaults, 15, 45, 65 and 95 ft, are the team's gate depths, read as feet below the water surface. Only the 45 ft gate is open, so everything Foothills measured came through it. You can change the other three.
    • Depth is below the water surface, and the surface moves with storage, so a real gate's depth drifts.
    • One sonde, one location. It was installed spring 2026 and the file covers 2026-04-07 to 08-19, with gaps.
    • "Best" is lowest turbidity, chlorophyll or phycocyanin, or highest oxygen. It isn't TOC, which the sonde doesn't measure.
    • Units marked "as logged" are as the file gives them. The sheet doesn't name them.

      How far back does the river tell you about the plant?

      Spearman correlation between an upstream reading L days earlier and the Foothills lab value. This is the signal clock. The water clock is about 4 hours from the sentinel gage to the intake, according to Denver Water's raw water group (guide.md §1).

      Lead time against accuracy

      Test error by horizon. Every model trains on the first half of the record and is tested on the second. Upstream-only = soft sensor from river, snow and season. Hybrid = last lab result + upstream changes.
